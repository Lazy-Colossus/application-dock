# Story 3.4: Light up the days that work

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As** anyone looking at the calendar,
**I want** the days most people can make to be the brightest thing on the screen,
**so that** the answer is obvious before I have read a single name.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **`washFor(free, ifNeeded, activeTotal)`** is pure and returns a step plus a provisional flag. (FR-14)
2. **Scaled against the active roster**, not an absolute count — five of six is near the top, five of ten is not. (FR-14)
3. **The top step means full coverage and nothing else**; one person short is always visibly below it, at every group size. (FR-14, NFR-6)
4. **Full coverage leaning on an if-needed** takes the same step, marked provisional by a hairline — never a fourth colour. (FR-14)
5. **Ink flips dark at the top two steps**, where the field is too light for pale text. (NFR-6)
6. **The count is in every cell** — colour is never the only signal. (NFR-6)

### Completion Notes

- **A test caught a real defect in my first scale.** Spreading coverage across all
  six steps with `ceil` made 7-of-8 and 8-of-8 land on the same step for any group
  larger than the ramp — collapsing exactly the distinction FR-14 exists to draw.
  The top step is now reserved for full coverage and everything short of it scales
  into 1–5.
- Monotonicity is asserted directly: one more person must never dim a day.
- Coverage above the roster size (possible for a moment after a removal, before a
  refetch) clamps to full rather than overflowing the ramp.
