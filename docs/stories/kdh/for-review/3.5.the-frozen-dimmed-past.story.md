# Story 3.5: The frozen, dimmed past

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As** anyone looking at the calendar,
**I want** past days to stay visible but plainly finished,
**so that** the grid is honest about what can still change without throwing away what happened.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **A past day keeps its votes, names, colours and wash**, at reduced emphasis. (FR-16)
2. **It is inert** — disabled, no sheet, no request; the client mirrors the server's rejection rather than relying on it. (FR-16)
3. **"Past" is the server's date**, never the device's, so a wrong or travelling clock cannot unfreeze a day for one person. (FR-16, NFR-5)
4. **A month entirely in the past** renders fully and readably.
5. **Today is not past** — it is votable and marked as today. (FR-16, FR-12)

### Completion Notes

- The boundary is a plain string comparison against `me.today`, which works
  because every date in this app is `YYYY-MM-DD` — lexical order is chronological
  order, and no timezone is involved anywhere.
- Enforced in three places on purpose: the cell is `disabled`, the grid never
  emits for it, and the server returns `422`. The first two are courtesy; the
  third is the guarantee.
- `today` is asserted as explicitly *not* past in both the backend and the page
  specs — it is the boundary case most likely to regress.
