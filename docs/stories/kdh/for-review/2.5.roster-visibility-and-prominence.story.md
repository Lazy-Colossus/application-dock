# Story 2.5: Make the roster visible and the name control unmissable

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As** anyone opening a calendar,
**I want** to see who is invited without opening anything, and to be told plainly when I have not said who I am,
**so that** the app does not sit quietly read-only while I wonder why nothing responds.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **List row.** The calendar's name carries a headcount beside it (person icon + number of active invitees); the subtext lists the invitees' names in roster order, trailing off past the sixth. A calendar with nobody on it says so. (FR-5)
2. **Summary contract.** `GET /api/kdh/calendars` returns `invitee_names` — the active roster in order — so the list needs no request per row. Tombstoned invitees appear in neither names nor count. (FR-5, AR-7)
3. **Calendar page headcount.** A headcount of active invitees sits beside the calendar's name.
4. **Unanswered name control.** Unclaimed, it is styled as a required field left blank — negative border and text, warning glyph — and is large enough to read as the first thing to answer. Claiming drops the treatment and leaves a quiet pill carrying that person's colour. (FR-10, FR-11)
5. **No regressions.** Suites green; linters and `vue-tsc` clean.
