# Story 2.4: Change your colour

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As** an invitee,
**I want** to swap my colour for another one that is free,
**so that** I am not stuck with a colour I cannot tell apart from someone else's.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Swatches live in the name dropdown**, below the roster — there is no separate colour surface. They appear only once a name is claimed. (FR-9)
2. **Taken colours are dimmed, not hidden**, so the palette keeps a stable shape; the claimed person's own colour is ringed. Tombstoned invitees count as holding their colour. (FR-9, AR-7)
3. **Picking a free colour** persists via `PUT /api/kdh/calendars/{id}/invitees/{inv}/color` and updates everywhere it appears, without a reload. (FR-9)
4. **Rejections.** A colour another invitee holds — tombstones included — or one outside the palette is `422`, and the chip keeps its previous colour. Re-picking your own colour is allowed and idempotent.
5. **Not admin-gated.** A guest may recolour the invitee they have claimed, because doing so is how they make the roster readable to themselves. (FR-3, AR-6)
6. **Unknown or removed invitee** is `404`.
7. **Serialized.** The read-modify-write runs inside `repo.calendar_transaction`. (NFR-1)

## Dev Notes

**This endpoint is deliberately not admin-gated**, unlike every other mutation so
far. The claim that says which invitee you are is not a security boundary anyway
(AR-6), and gating it would mean a guest could never fix a colour clash — which is
the entire point of the feature.

**The palette is duplicated across the stack** — `kdh_service.PALETTE` and
`types.ts` `INVITEE_PALETTE`. The server is authoritative and rejects anything not
in its own tuple, so a drift is a rejected request rather than a bad write; the
client copy exists only to render the swatches without an extra round trip.

### Completion Notes

- **Re-picking your own colour is explicitly allowed**, tested. The uniqueness
  check excludes the invitee being recoloured, so the swatch you already hold does
  not reject itself — an easy off-by-one that would look like a bug to a user
  tapping their own ring.
- Taken swatches are `disabled` as well as dimmed, and the click handler returns
  early on them, so a stray tap never fires a request that would `422`.
- **The duplicated palette is a real, if small, seam.** It is noted rather than
  solved: serving it from `GET /api/kdh/me` or a config endpoint would remove the
  duplication at the cost of a round trip before the dropdown can render. Worth
  revisiting if the palette ever changes again.
