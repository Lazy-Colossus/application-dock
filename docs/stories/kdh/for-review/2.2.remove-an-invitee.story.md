# Story 2.2: Remove an invitee without erasing the past

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As** an admin,
**I want** removing someone to clear only their upcoming availability,
**so that** a long-running calendar still records who was actually around for the sessions we played.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Future cleared, past kept.** `DELETE /api/kdh/calendars/{id}/invitees/{inv}` deletes that person's entries on dates **>= today** and leaves dates **< today** exactly as they were. Today counts as future — it is still votable. (FR-8)
2. **Emptied dates pruned.** A date left with an empty map is removed from `votes`, so no dead keys accumulate.
3. **Tombstone.** An invitee still holding past votes keeps their record with `name` and `color`, stamped `removed_at`. (AR-7)
4. **Outright drop.** An invitee holding no votes at all is deleted from the document entirely — the "added by mistake" case leaves no residue. (AR-7)
5. **`chosen_dates` untouched.** A session that happened still happened. (FR-8)
6. **Off the roster, colour still reserved.** A tombstone is excluded from the roster, the claimable names and the invitee count, but its colour is not handed to the next person added. (FR-8, FR-14, AR-7)
7. **Confirmation states the asymmetry.** The dialog names the person and says plainly that today onwards is cleared and the past is kept, before anything is written. (FR-8)
8. **Errors.** Removing an unknown or already-removed invitee is `404`; a guest is `403` with nothing written. (FR-3)
9. **Serialized.** The whole read-modify-write runs inside `repo.calendar_transaction`. (NFR-1)

## Dev Notes

**"Today" comes from `kdh_service.today()`**, never the client, and today is **not**
past — it is still votable, so a removal clears it. Tests drive that helper rather
than the clock.

**The endpoint returns the updated calendar**, not `204`, because the client needs
the new roster and vote map anyway and a second fetch would race the first.

### Completion Notes

- The three outcomes are distinguished by **whether any vote still references the
  invitee after the future is cleared** — tombstone if yes, drop if no. That single
  check covers both AC 3 and AC 4 without a special case.
- **Eight backend tests, each pinning one rule**, including the pair that looks
  contradictory: after removal a name may be reused but the colour may not. Both
  are asserted in one test so the asymmetry cannot silently drift.
- The confirmation copy states what is kept as well as what goes. This is the one
  destructive action in the app whose *partial* nature is the point, and a surprise
  is unrecoverable.
- `v-model="removingInvitee !== null"` is not valid Vue — the linter caught it. The
  dialog now binds a writable computed derived from which invitee is pending.
- **Suites:** backend **894** passed (was 886), frontend **1151** (was 1149).
