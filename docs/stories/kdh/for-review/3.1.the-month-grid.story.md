# Story 3.1: The month grid and moving between months

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As** an invitee,
**I want** the current month laid out as a grid I can page backwards and forwards through,
**so that** I can look at the days we might actually meet on.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Current month on load**, seven columns **Monday first**, correct leading blanks, every day of the month and no more. (FR-12)
2. **Arrows move one month**, across year boundaries in both directions. **No swipe** — on a 7×5 grid of tap targets it is too easily triggered while aiming for a Tuesday. (FR-12)
3. **A jump-back control** appears only once you have navigated away from the current month. (FR-12)
4. **Navigation makes no request** — it is a pure view change over the loaded document. (NFR-4)
5. **Month lengths are real** — 28, 29, 30 and 31-day months all render correctly.
6. **KDH's own theme**, scoped to `.kdh-app` so the shell and every other app keep Carbon.

### Completion Notes

- `css/kdh.sass` introduces the Eggplant Wash tokens on a `.kdh-app` root, the
  same seam Hotaru uses for Neon Yūgure. Nothing global changes.
- Monday-first is done by shifting `getDay()`, which is Sunday-first — the one
  arithmetic detail in the component, and tested by asserting the 1st of
  September 2026 (a Tuesday) sits after exactly one blank.
- The grid holds its own cursor and derives dates from it, so paging is free.
- **Prettier cannot parse indented `.sass`** — `hotaru.sass` has the same
  problem, so this is pre-existing rather than introduced here.
