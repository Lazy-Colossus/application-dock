# Story 3.3: The day sheet — who is on this date

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As** anyone looking at the calendar,
**I want** tapping a day to show me who can make it,
**so that** I can read the shape of a day without the names having to fit inside a 44px cell.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Tapping a day opens a sheet**, dismissible without changing anything. (FR-13)
2. **Only the people on that day**, in roster order, each with their colour as a **dot beside their name** — never as the row's background, so a person is never mistaken for a wash step. (FR-13, FR-9)
3. **If-needed rows are italic and recessed** — set apart by weight and style, never by tinting that person's colour. (FR-13, NFR-6)
4. **Someone since removed still appears** on a past day they answered. (FR-8, AR-7)
5. **A day nobody picked says so** rather than showing an empty list.
6. **Three explicit labelled controls** — Free / If needed / Can't — showing the current answer, because this is where a person deliberately answers. Absent on a past day and for anyone unclaimed. (FR-15)

### Completion Notes

- The sheet exists because the UX session found FR-13 impossible as written: a
  44px cell cannot hold six names, so the `+N` overflow would have been the
  permanent state. This is that resolution built.
- Rows are derived from `invitees` filtered by who has a vote, so a tombstoned
  person renders on the past days they answered without any special case.
- The grid keeps its cycling tap for a fast pass down a month; the sheet is
  explicit. Both write the same vote.
