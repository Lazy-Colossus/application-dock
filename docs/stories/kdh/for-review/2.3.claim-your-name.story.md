# Story 2.3: Claim your name on a calendar

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As** an invitee opening a shared link,
**I want** to say which of these people I am and have it remembered,
**so that** my clicks are mine without anybody needing an account.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **The control is in the header.** Unclaimed it reads "Who are you?"; open, it lists every **active** invitee as a row with their colour dot, each at least 44px tall. The month stays visible behind it. (FR-10)
2. **Claiming is remembered per calendar** in `localStorage` under `kdh.claim.{calendarId}`, and a later visit restores it with no re-pick. (FR-10)
3. **A stale or corrupt claim is discarded silently** — a claim naming a removed or unknown invitee resolves to unclaimed, the dead value is cleared from storage, and nothing is shown to the user. (FR-10, FR-8)
4. **Release and switch** are available at any time. (FR-10)
5. **Unclaimed means read-only.** The page exposes `canVote`, false until a name is claimed, which Story 3.2 consumes rather than re-deciding; and `promptForName`, which the month calls so an unclaimed tap **opens the dropdown** instead of being refused. (FR-11)
6. **Not a security boundary.** The claim is client-side only; the server validates only that an invitee exists and is active. (AR-6)
7. **Guests get this control** — claiming is not an admin action. (FR-3)
8. **Storage may be refused.** In private mode or with site data blocked, claiming still works for the page view and simply does not persist; nothing throws.

## Dev Notes

**`claimed` is derived, not stored.** The composable holds only the id; the invitee
is looked up in the live roster each time. A removed person therefore resolves to
`null` the instant the roster changes, with no invalidation step to forget.

**`restore()` is called after the calendar loads**, not on mount, so a stale claim
is resolved against a known roster rather than an empty one.

### Completion Notes

- Every `localStorage` access is wrapped: a browser that refuses site data throws
  on `getItem`/`setItem`, and this app's failure mode there should be "you re-pick
  your name" rather than a crash. Tested by making `Storage.prototype` throw.
- The stale-claim rule is enforced in two places on purpose: `claimed` resolves to
  `null` immediately (so the UI is correct on the first render), and a watcher
  clears the dead id from storage (so it is not carried around). Neither alone is
  sufficient.
- `canVote` and `promptForName` are exposed now, unused, because Story 3.2's ACs
  name them. They are the seam between this story and the month grid, and defining
  them here keeps 3.2 from re-deciding the rule.
- The dropdown deliberately shows for guests: claiming is how a guest becomes able
  to vote, so gating it on admin would make the app useless to the people it is for.
- **Suites:** backend **894** (untouched — this story is frontend only), frontend
  **1166** (was 1151).
