# Tea App — Idea Pool

Candidate features to consider when designing the tea app. Not yet scoped or
prioritised — this is the pool we draw from when we start real story work.

## Session-time (at the tea table)
- **Gongfu Session Timer** — per-infusion timing (steep #1, #2, #3…) that learns
  your curve and nudges each successive infusion longer the way you actually brew.
  Log water temp, vessel, leaf grams → brewing history per tea.
- **Steep Metronome** — a gentle visual/audio pulse for the pour-in-pour-out rhythm
  of fast gongfu infusions, so you're not squinting at a stopwatch mid-pour.

## Journaling & reflection
- **Cha Xi Journal** — document each tea sitting as an aesthetic object: tea, ware
  used (yixing pot, gaiwan, chawan), mood, guests, photo of the setup. Builds a
  visual archive of your table. (Optional: per-pot seasoning log.)
- **Tea Wheel** — a flavor/aroma tasting wheel you tap through during a session
  (honey, petrichor, roasted, marine, camphor…), saving a flavor fingerprint per
  tea for later side-by-side comparison.
- **Tea Meditation Timer** — steep + a silent sitting period, framed as mindfulness
  practice. Brew, then breathe while it cools.

## Collection & knowledge
- **The Cabinet** — inventory of teas with grams remaining, auto-decremented as you
  log sessions, plus a "running low" shelf. (Generalises the Pu-erh cellar / aging idea.)
- **Tea Map** — where each tea comes from (mountain, village, region) pinned on a map.
  A map of everywhere your shelf has "traveled."
- **Cha Dao Almanac** — a quiet curated reference: brewing parameters, tea families,
  ceremony vocabulary (Chinese + Japanese terms). A personal tea encyclopedia.

## Notes
- Suggested sequencing (rough): Session Timer → Cha Xi Journal → The Cabinet →
  Tea Wheel → the rest.
