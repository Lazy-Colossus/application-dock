# Story 2.6: Favourites

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

As a cook,
I want the recipes I actually cook kept at the top,
so that my weeknight staples are always one tap away.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Toggle from the list, in place.** Given a recipe in the collection list, when its favourite toggle is tapped, then the flag persists, the mark fills immediately, and the action neither navigates away, nor loses scroll position, **nor moves the row**. (FR-13)

2. **Favourites sort above everything, on load.** Given a collection containing favourites, when it is **listed** — on first load or any refetch — then favourites sort above everything else, with creation-date descending within each group, completing the ordering left partial by Story 1.4. The order is settled at load and is **not** re-derived when a favourite is toggled, so a recipe favourited now joins the favourites group on the next load. (FR-10, FR-13)

3. **Still marked inside filtered results.** Given an active filter or search, when results are shown, then favourites among them remain visibly marked. (FR-13)

4. **A favourites-only view, one interaction away.** Given the collection screen, when the user wants only favourites, then a favourites-only view is reachable in one interaction. (FR-13)

### Completion Notes

- **The toggle is optimistic and does NOT re-sort.** As shipped it did: `toggleFavourite` called `sortRecipes` on all three of its paths, so the mark filled and the row jumped to the favourites group. That was this story's reading of Flow 5's climax, and in use it was the wrong one — the row leaves from under the finger that tapped it and the reader loses their place in the list. Reversed in `3cf8565`. `setRating` had already refused to re-sort for exactly this reason (Story 2.7, AC 4); the two now agree, and "the scroll position does not move" is now literally true rather than true-of-the-viewport-only.
- **The ordering itself is unchanged and still belongs to the server.** `_collection_order` lifts favourites on every read and `sortRecipes` mirrors it on fetch, create and update. What changed is only *when* the client re-derives it: at load, not on tap. A spec pins that favourites still come first after a refetch, because that is the half of AC 2 that must not rot.
- **`toggleFavourite` is intentionally excluded from `loading`.** Every other action in the store sets it; this one must not, because the collection binds spinner and disabled state to `loading` and the user may be mid-scroll. A spec asserts `loading` stays `false` throughout. This is the deviation from the store contract most likely to look like an oversight in review — it is not.
- **The optimistic path is asserted against a promise that never settles**, so the test proves the mark filled *before* the response, not merely after it.
- **The mark is a shape change in ink, not a colour change** (UX-DR17): filled versus outlined, legible with colour off entirely. It wore a star here and moved to the heart in Story 2.7, once the rating took the star.
- **The "never moss" half of that rule is retired.** Moss meant "pressable" across the dock, so a status mark could not wear it. The Notebook retheme (`798df7e`) removed moss, and its burgundy is both the marking pen and the interactive colour — so the colour clause no longer has anything to say and the shape clause carries the whole guarantee. `RecipeRow.spec.ts` asserted `not.toMatch(/--kc-moss/)`, which the retheme quietly made vacuous; it now asserts the mark is a glyph and not a chip fill, which is the part that still has to hold.
- **This story completes the ordering Story 1.4 left partial.** `_collection_order` uses two stable passes rather than one composite key, because the date leg is descending and the favourite leg ascending; the document index breaks exact-timestamp ties.
- **Favourites-only is a filter-bar toggle, not a route** — the spine's own open question. It satisfies "one interaction from the collection" but is not deep-linkable. Left as the spine assumed; a route would be the alternative if shareable URLs ever matter.
- The favourites-only empty state has **no button**, per UX-DR13. Worth noting because every other empty state in the app has one.
