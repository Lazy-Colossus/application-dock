# Story 2.8: Ingredients become amount + unit + name

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

As a cook,
I want an ingredient to be a quantity, a unit and a thing,
so that my recipes record what I actually need to buy and cook with.

## Context

This **replaces** the two-level ingredient tag introduced in Story 2.3. It is a
deletion as much as an addition, decided with the blast radius stated in
advance:

| Removed | Was |
|---|---|
| The pantry filter | FR-12, Story 2.5 |
| The 105-category shipped vocabulary | UX-DR19 |
| The coining ceremony | FR-9/FR-22, Story 2.3 AC 3 |
| Story 4.4, *"Keep the ingredient vocabulary from drifting"* | Epic 4 |
| Category-based staple detection | Story 3.3 |

The schema goes to v2 and existing documents migrate on read.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Three parts, one required.** An ingredient is `amount`, `unit` and `text`. Only `text` is required — `garlic` is a complete ingredient — because no optional field may stand between a paste and a saved recipe (FR-3).

2. **Amount is free text, never parsed.** `1/2`, `2-3` and `a few` are all things cooks write. Nothing converts, rounds or validates them, on the same principle that keeps the body verbatim.

3. **Units suggest without closing.** A shipped list is offered; anything typed is accepted and joins that user's own vocabulary. A native `datalist`, which is exactly "offer a list, accept anything".

4. **Ingredients suggest from the user's own history.** There is no shared vocabulary in v2, so the field offers what this cook has typed before — which is what keeps `Feta` and `feta` from becoming two things.

5. **One per line, everywhere.** The reading view and the edit screen both list ingredients one per line in entry order, with amount and unit in their own right-aligned column so quantities line up down the list.

6. **Absence renders as absence, inside the line.** An ingredient with no amount and no unit is just its name — no gap, no dash, no placeholder (NFR-8).

7. **The same ingredient may appear twice.** `100 g butter` for the pastry and `20 g butter` for the pan are two entries. Only entries identical in all three parts are dropped.

8. **Existing recipes migrate without reading differently.** A v1 tag displayed as its specific where there was one and its bare category where there was not, so `text = specific or category` leaves every recipe reading exactly as it did. Amount and unit start empty; the category is dropped.

9. **Nothing survives that depended on categories.** No pantry filter, no shared vocabulary, no coining row, no `categories` on the document, and no `ingredient:{category}` provenance keys.

10. **Staples still work, and still under-match.** Salt and black pepper start unchecked when sending a recipe to the shopping list, matched on the full text, exactly and case-insensitively.

### Completion Notes

- **The migration is display-equivalence, not information-preservation.** `text = specific or category` is lossy — `chicken → thighs` becomes `thighs`, losing "chicken" — but it is the only rule that guarantees no recipe reads differently after the upgrade than before it. Verified against the real 13-recipe collection: 97 rows, none dropped.
- **The staple match moved from category to text, and got narrower.** It was an exact match on a category from a closed vocabulary; it is now an exact match on free text. `red peppers` and `salted butter` stay checked, and so does `freshly ground black pepper` — it under-matches rather than over-matches, because a staple left checked costs one unticked line while a real ingredient silently dropped costs the meal. Three tests name those cases.
- **`TypeaheadInput` lost the coining ceremony entirely.** With the shared vocabulary gone, Tags is its only caller and nothing passed `allowCoin`, so the whole path was dead code. Removed rather than left, per the repo's no-dead-code rule.
- **Ingredients use a native `datalist` rather than the custom typeahead.** "Offer a list, accept anything" is precisely what a datalist is, and it needs no coining concept.
- **The two documents now version independently.** The collection is v2, the shopping list is still v1, and `migrate()` takes the expected version rather than assuming one constant covers both.
- **The shipped seed changed job**, from 105 ingredient categories to 22 units. Same read-only-in-the-image pattern.
- **This fixes the "smoked" problem** Story 3.3 flagged: with amount and unit on the ingredient, a cook writes `2 tsp smoked paprika` and the shopping list receives exactly that.

### Open Questions

- **The PRD and both UX spines still describe the two-level model.** FR-7, FR-8, FR-9, FR-12, FR-22, UX-DR19 and Story 4.4 are all now wrong. They were not amended here — that is a documentation pass of its own, and it should be done deliberately rather than as a side effect of a code change.
- **Migrated ingredients that lost their noun.** `smoked` and `dried` survive from v1 specifics that were adjectives. Nothing can recover the category now; they need editing by hand, or leaving.
