# Story 1.4: Arrange furniture in layouts

## Status
Ready for Review

## Story
**As** Jake or Dani,
**I want** to drag our furniture around the locked plan in a few named layouts,
**so that** we can compare arrangements and pick the best one before moving day.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Arrange mode is only available while the plan is locked. While the plan is unlocked, it shows
   a prompt to lock the plan first (FP-8).
2. Layouts appear as tabs in the bottom bar. You can add, rename, duplicate and delete layouts,
   but the last one cannot be deleted. A new apartment starts with "Layout A" (LA-1).
3. The left panel shows the tray of pieces not in this layout, with the pieces already on the plan
   below it. Dragging a piece from the tray onto the plan places it, and **Back to tray** removes
   it from the layout. Each piece can be placed at most once per layout (LA-2, LA-3).
4. Pieces snap to 10 cm while dragged. **R**, or the Left/Right buttons, rotate the selected piece
   by 90° around its centre (LA-4).
5. The right panel shows the selected piece's name, shape, colour, size in cm, position in metres
   and rotation (LA-5).
6. A piece that overlaps a wall, covers an empty square, or overlaps another piece's bounding box
   gets a dashed orange outline, and the right panel shows a warning. The move is never blocked
   (LA-5).
7. Each place, move or rotate is saved as its own write, so two people arranging different pieces
   don't refuse each other's changes (NFR-a).
8. `geometry.ts` (rotated bounds, snapping, overlap checks) has a spec file. The backend has tests
   for placing a piece on an unlocked plan, placing it twice, duplicating a layout, and deleting
   the last layout.

### Completion Notes
- Built per the plan, Tasks 1–8.
- **Server rules:**
  - Placements are upserted, so a piece appears at most once per layout.
  - Placing, moving and sending a piece back to the tray all need a locked plan.
  - A piece's centre must stay on the plan, and shrinking the plan prunes placements left off it.
  - All arranging writes are last-write-wins and never touch `plan_rev`.
- **Frontend:**
  - `geometry.ts` holds rotated bounds (turning about the centre), 10 cm snapping, and the three
    LA-5 warnings judged on the bounding box. Pieces that only touch, overlapping by 1 cm or less,
    don't warn.
  - Placements update on screen before the server answers; a refused write refetches.
  - `LabelDialog` became `NameDialog`, which is shared by room labels and layout names.
- **Spec changes:** LA-3 now has Back to tray as a button only; dragging a piece back onto the
  tray is out of scope. LA-5 records the 1 cm touch tolerance and the centre-on-plan rule.
- **Found in the browser check, fixed in the follow-up commit:** a piece moved later was drawn on
  top and hid the coffee table beneath it. The selected piece is now always drawn last.
- **Browser check** (Playwright, 1440 × 900, a walled two-room flat with six pieces):
  - A tray drop lands centred under the cursor, snapped.

  - The wardrobe across the bedroom wall shows the dashed outline and "Overlaps a wall".
  - Names stay upright on rotated pieces, and the white bookshelf is visible.
  - Back to tray works.
  - "+ New layout" makes an empty Layout B; Duplicate and Rename give "Sofa by the window" with
