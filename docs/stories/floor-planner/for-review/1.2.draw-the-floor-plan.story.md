# Story 1.2: Draw the floor plan

## Status
Ready for Review

## Story
**As** Jake or Dani,
**I want** to paint our apartment roughly onto a 20 cm grid, seeing real sizes as I go,
**so that** the plan is close enough to scale to trust furniture placed on it.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. The plan is drawn in centimetres. It has metre rulers along the top and left edges, a thin line
   on every square, a darker line every metre, and the scale card from design B ("1 square =
   20 cm" with a 1 m bar). Zooming in or out never changes the stored geometry (NFR-c).
2. Brushes:
   - Structure brushes: Wall, Window, Door, Front door.
   - Floor brushes, each with inline chips that start on the default:
     - Tile: white*, grey, beige, black
     - Wood: light, medium*, dark
     - Carpet: grey*, beige, blue, green
     - Balcony
   - Tools: Eraser and Room label.

   A structure brush paints the feature layer and a floor brush paints the surface layer. A wall
   hides the floor under it, and the eraser clears both layers (FP-3, FP-4).
3. Every brush works in **Freehand** mode (click or drag across squares) and **Rectangle** mode
   (drag a box to fill it) (FP-4).
4. While you draw, a dark chip next to the shape and the status bar both show its size, for
   example "Tile · 1.60 × 2.60 m". The status bar always shows the cursor position in metres
   (FP-5).
5. Room labels can be added by clicking the plan with the Label tool, and renamed, moved and
   deleted. They are listed in the right panel (FP-6).
6. Undo and redo cover the current editing session, and one stroke or rectangle counts as one
   step (FP-7).
7. The plan can be resized by width and depth in metres, rounded up to whole squares, up to 30 m
   on each side. Growing adds empty squares on the right and bottom edges, and shrinking crops
   them (FP-2).
8. Changes stay local until **Save** or **Lock plan**. The status bar shows that there are unsaved
   changes, and leaving the page with unsaved changes asks for confirmation. Locking the plan
   saves any unsaved changes first. Painting is refused on a locked plan (FP-8). Lock and Unlock
   themselves were built in 1.1.
9. The pure helpers in `grid.ts` (encode/decode, paint, fill rectangle, resize) have spec files.
   The backend has tests for painting while locked and for the size bounds.

### Completion Notes
- Built per the plan.
- `history.ts` is pure functions (`push`/`undo`/`redo`), not a stateful object, so it sits
  cleanly in Pinia refs.
- The pointer-to-square maths (`cellAt`) and label hit-testing (`labelAtCell`) live in
  `grid.ts`, so they are tested without a DOM.
- **Two bugs found only by driving the app in Chromium (Playwright), both fixed in `5e0ed73`:**
  1. The label dialog took no clicks. Quasar gives pointer events only to a dialog's direct
     `div` children, and the dialog's root was a `<form>`. The unit tests stub `q-dialog`, so
     they could not catch it.
  2. The page grew past the window, pushing the status bar (Save, cursor, zoom) off screen. The
     page now sizes to the window with `q-page`'s `style-fn`, and the panels and plan scroll
     inside it. The body row no longer wraps, because a wrapped row sizes to its content.
- **Browser check** (1440 × 900, `jake`/`dani` on a throwaway `DATA_DIR`):
  - The rectangle readout reads "Wood · 3.20 × 2.60 m".
  - A fast freehand wall has no gaps, and its readout reads "Wall · 8.20 m".
  - A label can be added; Save, then Lock with unsaved changes, saves and then locks.
  - A locked plan disables every control.
  - Conflict: Dani has unsaved carpet and Jake saves first. Dani sees "jake changed the plan…"
    with Discard, and her drawing is kept. Save again overwrites, keeping her carpet.
  - No console errors.
- Gate:
  - Frontend: 2812 tests, lint, vue-tsc and build are all clean.
  - Backend: the floor-planner tests pass. The 6 Tea `JWT_SECRET_KEY` failures predate this work.
