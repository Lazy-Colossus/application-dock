are distilled from this at Finalize; both win over any mock on conflict.

**Stakes:** hobby — six friends, self-hosted, no compliance surface.
**Mode:** Create. Workspace bound 2026-09-03.

## Entry 1 — Workspace naming deviates from the skill pattern

The configured `run_folder_pattern` is `ux-{project_name}-{date}`, which would
give `ux-application-dock-2026-09-03` and read as a second platform-wide run.

`epics-kdh.md` is named. Pattern deviation is deliberate.

## Entry 3 — FINDING: the "platform spine" is not a platform spine

`ux-designs/ux-application-dock-2026-06-10/` is named for the platform but both
its files are titled **Hotaru** and describe **Neon Yūgure** — a deep-indigo
dusk field with cyan/magenta/violet neon, violet glass panels, drifting
fireflies, **phone-viewport only, full-bleed**. That is one app's identity, not
the dock's.

**KDH therefore inherits nothing from it.** What KDH actually inherits is the
shell's own theme, which lives in code rather than in any UX document:

`frontend/src/css/quasar.variables.sass` — **"Carbon"**
| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `surface-base` | `#141414` | near-black page |
| `surface-raised` | `#1E1E1E` | raised surface |
| `surface-card` | `#242424` | card fill |
| `ink-primary` | `#F0F0F0` | body text |
| `ink-secondary` | `#8A8A8A` | secondary text |
| `ink-disabled` | `#4A4A4A` | disabled |
| `accent` (= `primary`) | `#C8960A` | muted gold — the single accent |
| `border-color` | `#2A2A2A` | hairline borders |
| `confirmed` / `positive` | `#2E7D32` | success |
| `error` / `negative` | `#CF6679` | error |

Consequence for this session: the coverage heat ramp has to live inside a
**near-black, single-gold-accent** world, not a light one. Any palette decision
is made against `#141414`, and gold is already spoken for as "the interactive
accent" across the dock.

## Entry 4 — GAP: form factor was never specified

The KDH design spec states no form factor. The two readings in it pull in
opposite directions: "unmistakable across a room" implies a wide screen, while
six people voting from a shared link will realistically do it on phones. Every
other decision in Epic 3 — how many names fit in a cell, whether `+N` overflow
is the common case or the exception, whether the heat ramp or the count is the
primary signal — depends on this. Must resolve before IA closes.

Note: Listies pinned this explicitly (NFR-1, desktop/wide-first). KDH did not.

## Entry 5 — DECIDED: phone-first, one layout

Everyone does everything on a phone, admins included. One layout, no desktop
variant. Matches Hotaru's form factor and the reality of six people opening a
link from a group chat.

## Entry 6 — CONSEQUENCE: FR-13 as written cannot hold

FR-13 says *"Each day cell lists, in small text, the names of the invitees on
it"*, with a `+N` remainder above an overflow threshold. That was written
assuming a wide cell. A phone month is seven columns across ~360px — roughly
**48px per cell**. Names do not fit; even one 4-letter name at a legible size
is marginal, and six is impossible.

So on a phone the `+N` overflow is not the exception, it is **always** the case,
which makes the whole names-in-cell mechanic dead weight.

**Resolution to confirm with the user:** the cell carries the signals that
survive at 48px — the date, the coverage **count**, and the **heat** — and the
names move to a **day detail** surface opened by tapping the cell, which is
also where a claimed person sets their own availability. This turns FR-13 from
"names in the cell" into "names one tap away", and adds a surface the IA did
not previously have.

Knock-on: NFR-6's *"unmistakable across a room"* is a desktop idea. On a phone
the bar is *readable at arm's length*, one-handed, outdoors.

## Entry 7 — DECIDED: its own identity, but very minimal

Not the dock's Carbon theme, and not a Hotaru-scale expressive world. KDH gets
its own visual language whose defining quality is **restraint** — few elements,
generous space, and colour used sparingly enough that the heat ramp is the
loudest thing on the screen.

Open: field colour (light or dark), and how coverage is expressed. Being
elicited via rendered directions rather than described in prose.

## Entry 8 — DECIDED: the "Wash" encoding

Coverage is the cell's background; the month reads as a field where the good
days are brighter. Chosen over "Fill" (a vessel filling from the bottom) and
"Ring" (a dial per day).

Accepted trade-offs, recorded so they are not rediscovered as bugs:
- Four-of-six and five-of-six are hard to separate by wash alone. **The count
  stays in the cell** to settle it — it is not decoration.
- Wash leans hardest of the three on colour, so the count and the chosen-day
  shape are what carry the meaning for a colour-blind reader.

## Entry 9 — DECIDED: purple world — dark ground, eggplant → pastel ramp

Wash re-skinned at the user's direction: violet-black field, the ramp running
from deep eggplant at low coverage to pastel lilac at full.

## Entry 10 — CONSEQUENCE: the invitee palette must leave the purple family

Two different things are encoded by colour and they must not be confused:
- **the ramp** answers *how many can come* — purple, ordered, low to high;
- **an invitee colour** answers *which person* — categorical, unordered.

If invitees were also purple, a person's colour would read as a coverage level.
So invitee colours become **varied pastels** — rose, sky, mint, sand, lilac,
aqua — desaturated enough to sit on the violet ground without competing with
the ramp for attention.

This obsoletes the 12-hue palette currently shipped in
`kdh_service.PALETTE` (ember, cobalt, violet, jade, magenta, amber…), which was
picked before any visual direction existed and is too saturated for this world.
Replacing it is a small change to one tuple, but it is a change to code already
merged in Story 1.3 — noted for Finalize.

## Entry 11 — DECIDED: header dropdown for claiming a name

Chosen over the bottom sheet (biggest targets, but covers the month) and the
chip rail (never hides the roster, but eats permanent vertical space and wraps
badly past ~8 people).

Consequences:
- The month stays visible while picking — the reason it won.
- It is the natural home for the colour swatches, so **FR-9 recolouring lives
  inside the same control** rather than needing a surface of its own.
- Targets are the smallest of the three, so the rows must be at least 44px and
  the control must not sit flush against the top edge.

## Entry 12 — ASSUMPTION: low end of the ramp needs more separation

Not confirmed by the user. Steps 0 (`#221B2E`) and 1 (`#2B2040`) are close
enough that "one person is free" may read as "nobody". Step 1 is lightened to
`#31234A` in the spine so the first vote is visibly a change; the count is the
backstop either way. Overturnable.

## Entry 13 — Upstream reconciled to the spines

The user ruled that this session's decisions take precedence over the documents
written before it. Changes made:

**`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-02-kdh-design.md`**
- **NFR-0 added** — phone-only, one layout. The spec previously stated no form
  factor at all; this is the constraint the rest follows from.
- **FR-13 rewritten** — names moved from the cell to the day sheet.
- **NFR-6 rewritten** — "unmistakable across a room" became "at arm's length,
  one-handed, on a phone", plus the explicit 4.5:1 contrast floor.
- *Interaction design* and *Visual direction* rewritten to match, both now
  deferring to the spines as the winning contract.
- Frontend structure: `InviteePanel` → `NameDropdown`, `AdminBar` → `AdminMenu`,
  and `DaySheet` added.

**`docs/planning-artifacts/epics-kdh.md`**
- Same FR-13 / NFR-6 / NFR-0 changes; UX spines added to `inputDocuments`.
- **Story 3.3 rewritten** from "See who is on each day" (names in the cell) to
  "The day sheet — who is on this date".
- Stories 2.3 and 2.4 now specify the header dropdown and put the colour
  swatches inside it; 3.1 records arrows-only; 3.2 says the cell carries date and
  count only, and that an unclaimed tap opens the dropdown; 3.4 and 4.1 name the
  actual tokens and marks.
- `useHeatScale` → `useWashScale` throughout, matching the identity.

**`backend/app/services/kdh_service.py`**
- `PALETTE` replaced: twelve saturated hues → the eight pastels, with a comment
  recording that invitee colours are categorical and must stay outside the wash's
  purple family. Backend 863 and frontend 1131 stay green — the palette's tests
  assert uniqueness, ordering and exhaustion rather than specific values, so they
  covered the swap without modification.
