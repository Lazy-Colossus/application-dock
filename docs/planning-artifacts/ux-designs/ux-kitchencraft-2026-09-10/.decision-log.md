Canonical record of what was decided and why. `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md`
are distilled from this at Finalize; both win over any mock on conflict.

**Stakes:** hobby — private household collection, single-user per dock account,
no compliance surface.
**Mode:** Create. Workspace bound 2026-09-10.
**Working mode:** Fast path — gaps batched, spines drafted with `[ASSUMPTION]`
tags for anything not stated, creative tools skipped unless a layout question
forces one.

## Entry 3 — Visual language: own identity, dock chassis

KitchenCraft gets a distinct palette and type treatment of its own rather than
reusing another app's tokens, but stays inside the same shell layout and the
same component set (Vue 3 + Quasar v2, Material-based).

**Why:** this is the established house pattern, not a departure from it. The
dock *does* have a real shell identity — **"Carbon"**, in
`ux-designs/ux-Code-2026-05-28/DESIGN.md` (status `final`, mirrored 1:1 in
`frontend/src/css/quasar.variables.sass`). But every app spine so far
deliberately overrides it: Hotaru declares itself a brand-layer delta over the
Quasar system, and KDH states outright that it is "deliberately not the dock's
Carbon theme: KDH is its own thing, the way Hotaru is." So Carbon is the
chassis-level fallback that wins wherever an app spine is silent, and the app
spine wins where it speaks.

*(Correction: an earlier reading of this workspace held that no dock token set
existed to inherit, on the strength of `ux-application-dock-2026-06-10` being
Hotaru's "Neon Yūgure" under a platform-sounding folder name. That folder is
indeed mis-named, but Carbon is the genuine shell spine and it lives in the
`ux-Code-2026-05-28` folder — also mis-named. Both folder names mislead; the
document titles are authoritative.)*

## Entry 4 — Form factor: phone and desktop as peers

Both surfaces designed properly, neither as a fallback.

**Why:** capture and the bulk enrichment pass are desktop-weighted work,
mid-cook reading is phone with wet hands.

**This extends the PRD rather than implementing it.** The specs are
unambiguously phone-only: NFR-4 asks for "every screen usable one-handed at
phone width", the success metric is "readable at arm's length on a phone,
one-handed, without pinch-zoom", and **no desktop or wide layout requirement,
and no breakpoint guidance, appears anywhere in the PRD or epics**. Designing
desktop as a peer is net-new scope beyond NFR-4 — it needs breakpoints, a wide
layout for the collection and reading view, and a desktop posture for the
shopping-list modal that phone-only would not have required.

Note also that Epic 4 (enrichment) contributes **zero UI** — it is a
`backend/scripts/` script run from Claude Code, with no HTTP surface. So the
desktop-weighted half of the rationale rests on capture, not enrichment; the
enrichment pass never becomes a screen in v1.

## Entry 5 — The PRD already carries an aesthetic brief

NFR-8 and PRD §11 are not silent on identity, so the spines inherit rather
than invent:

- "Calm and uncluttered — **a notebook, not a magazine**."
- "The text is the hero: generous line height, comfortable measure, no
  decoration competing with the method while someone is cooking from it."
- "Structure (tags, time, meal type) is present but quiet, sitting at the
  edges rather than framing the content."
- "Empty fields are invisible, never rendered as gaps waiting to be filled —
  the app must never make an unstructured recipe feel unfinished."

Counter-metric with direct UX bite: "capture friction reintroduced by field
creep."

## Entry 6 — OPEN: ground tone unresolved (blocks the palette)

Every existing app spine is a dark field (Carbon `#141414`, Hotaru
`#0a0a24`-ish, KDH `#15111C`), and Carbon's rationale is "dark surfaces
disappear in outdoor light". But KitchenCraft's own brief is "a notebook, not a
magazine" with text as the hero — and a notebook is paper. This is the one
identity decision that cannot be assumed without picking a side, because it
determines the whole palette and every contrast pair.

Awaiting the user. Not resolvable from the sources.

## Entry 12 — The two PRD open items, resolved

**Ingredient category vocabulary: ships seeded.** A starter list of common
categories ships with the app, so the Ingredients typeahead and the pantry
filter both do useful work against the very first recipe rather than being
inert surfaces.

- Closes the Entry 7 open item, and removes a day-one dead-weight state from
  two surfaces.
- There is direct precedent in this repo: Hotaru ships a vocabulary seed
  (see commits `24422ce` "add the Kaishi 1.5k deck to the shipped vocabulary
  seed" and `f95697c` "pour a Japanese tea glossary into the shipped seed"),
  so a shipped seed is an established platform pattern, not a new mechanism.
- Consequence for EXPERIENCE.md: the Ingredients input has no true empty
  state on first run, but a genuinely new category must still be committable
  and must still read as "a visibly distinct act from picking an existing
  one" (PRD FR-5 / Story 2.3).
- Consequence for Epic 4: `4.4 Keep the ingredient vocabulary from drifting`
  now guards a seeded vocabulary rather than bootstrapping one.
- Residual risk the user accepted: a shipped taxonomy is someone else's
  taxonomy. Mitigation is that the seed is a starting point, not a closed
  list.

**Provenance: quietly marked.** A value written by the enrichment script
carries a subtle tell that reads as *unconfirmed* — a lighter ink or a
hairline outline rather than a filled chip — without shouting. Touching or
editing it makes it the user's own and the tell drops.

- This **turns PRD FR-19 from an open architecture question into a
  requirement**: provenance must be stored per structured field and exposed
  through the API, not merely tracked inside the enrichment script.
- It needs a DESIGN.md token pair (confirmed chip vs unconfirmed chip) and an
  EXPERIENCE.md state transition (unconfirmed → confirmed on user touch).
- The heavier option — a bulk review-and-confirm surface — was offered and
  **not** taken, consistent with the PRD's scoped-small posture. No new
  screen is added.

## Entry 13 — VOID (direction "Blockcut", amended with moss accent and texture)

Struck at the user's instruction, 2026-09-10, along with both rendered
exploration files. Superseded by Entry 14.

**Retained as reference data, explicitly NOT as decisions** — these were
measured, not preferred, and re-measuring them later would be waste:

| pair | ratio | |
|---|---|---|
| `#5D8A46` fill + `#F4F2EC` text | 3.61 | fails AA |
| `#5D8A46` fill + `#0D0D0C` text | 4.80 | AA |
| `#5D8A46` hairline on `#F4F2EC` | 3.61 | passes 3:1 non-text |
| `#0D0D0C` on `#F4F2EC` | 17.37 | AAA |
| `#0D0D0C` on `#E7E4DA` | 15.28 | AAA |
| `#86867E` on `#F4F2EC` | 3.28 | fails |
| `#64645C` on `#F4F2EC` | 5.33 | AA |

The transferable lesson, independent of palette: **a mid-lightness accent
cannot carry pale text.** Any accent light enough to read as "light" will need
ink-coloured text on its fills, or it must be reserved for hairlines, marks and
state rather than for filled buttons.

## Entry 14 — RESET. Clean typographic. Supersedes Entries 10, 11 and 13.

Two rounds of visual exploration were rejected outright ("Remove all of these
and lets start again", "Remove everything"), and both rendered files were
deleted at the user's instruction. The illustrative ambition is dropped.

**Diagnosis, recorded so it is not repeated.** The brief asked for a mood
carried almost entirely by imagery, and across two rounds every carrier of it
was removed in turn — texture, then carved shapes, then texture again as
CSS-only gradients, then all icons and illustration. Each removal was
reasonable alone; together they left the mood with nothing to ride on. CSS
hatching at 6% opacity is not a linocut. The lesson is that an illustrative
register cannot be reached by prose-to-HTML inference; it needs either real
artwork or reference images, and neither was in scope.

**The brief now, in full:**

- **Clean typographic.** Paper ground, near-black ink, one accent, good type.
- **No imagery.** No illustration, no texture, no decorative icons, no
  ornament. Nothing drawn.
- Light ground stands — this **resolves Entry 6** (ground tone) on its own
  terms rather than by inheriting the struck linocut rationale. KitchenCraft
  remains the first light-ground app on a dock of dark-field apps.

**The PRD's own §11 is now the whole aesthetic brief**, which it is well suited
to be, being a requirement rather than a style preference:

- "Calm and uncluttered — a notebook, not a magazine."
- "The text is the hero: generous line height, comfortable measure, no
  decoration competing with the method while someone is cooking from it."
- "Structure (tags, time, meal type) is present but quiet, sitting at the
  edges rather than framing the content."
- "Empty fields are invisible, never rendered as gaps waiting to be filled —
  the app must never make an unstructured recipe feel unfinished."

There is no longer any tension between the identity and §11. The collision
logged in Entry 10 is dissolved by the reset, not managed.

### Consequences, stated so they can be struck

- **Microcopy goes plain.** "Light seasoning" (struck Entry 11 item 3) needed
  an alchemical register to season; with that gone it has no referent. Plain
  English throughout — `Ingredients`, `Tags`, `Shopping list`, and the FR-4
  meal types verbatim.
- **Empty and zero-result states are now purely typographic.** They still must
  not be bare panels (PRD FR-8: the empty collection "shows a prompt to
  capture the first recipe, not a blank screen"; FR-13: the empty list "says
  so plainly and offers hand-entry"). Type, rule, spacing and copy carry them.
  One device from the deleted work is worth keeping on its merits, being
  behavioural rather than visual: **the zero-result state lists each active
  filter with its own count** ("chickpeas — 7 on its own"), so it explains why
  nothing matched instead of only offering to clear filters. Goes to
  EXPERIENCE.md.
- **Functional icons are a chassis question, not an identity one.** With no
  decorative iconography to conflict with, Quasar's Material icons are
  available for touch targets (the shopping-list button, search, back) on the
  same footing as the rest of the dock, whose card for this app is `menu_book`.
  Drafted as `[ASSUMPTION]`: functional icons yes, decorative none.
- **Accent scope.** Per the platform rule KDH states — the accent means
  "clickable" across the dock — the single accent is reserved for interactive
  and active state. Favourites and the ticked-item mark are carried in ink.
  Drafted as `[ASSUMPTION]`.
- Exact paper, ink, accent and type values are drafted as `[ASSUMPTION]` in
  DESIGN.md for the user to strike, per fast-path working mode. No further
  rendered exploration unless asked.

## Entry 15 — Palette and type specified. Amends Entry 14.

User verbatim: "Lets keep the design more simple, lets go with beige
background and black n moss green n pumpkin taupe tones. Lets try and keep it
folk.witchcraft looking with serif font or sth very simple"

**The folk register is partially reinstated, and it is worth being precise
about which half.** Entry 10 (void) bundled two things together: a *mood*
(fantasy/alchemical, folk, witchcraft) and a *delivery mechanism* (linocut,
woodcut, texture, carved illustration). The mechanism is what failed twice and
stays dead. The mood returns, carried now by **palette and type only** — the
word used was "looking". Entry 14's no-imagery constraint is untouched and
absolute: no illustration, no texture, no grain, no ornament, no decorative
icons, nothing drawn.

**Palette — user-specified, no longer the designer's to propose:**

- Ground: **beige** — warmer and more saturated than the newsprint off-white
  that Entry 14 implied.
- **Black** — ink: text, the favourites mark, the ticked-item strike, all
  structural rules.
- **Moss green** — interactive and active state only: selected filters,
  primary buttons, focus rings. Keeps the platform rule that the accent means
  "clickable" across the dock.
- **Pumpkin taupe** — the quiet register: hairlines, meta text, structure at
  the edges, and specifically the "quietly marked" *unconfirmed* provenance
  tell from Entry 12, which is exactly the kind of not-shouting job it suits.

The one-accent rule from Entry 14 is superseded: there are now two accents
plus ink. The allocation above gives each tone exactly one job, and is drafted
as `[ASSUMPTION]` for the user to strike.

**Contrast risk, flagged before values are chosen:** pumpkin taupe is the
exposed one. If it does not clear 4.5:1 on beige it must not carry small body
or meta text and is restricted to hairlines, marks and large text at the 3:1
non-text bar. The resolution is to **restrict the use, not to shift the hue** —
the tones are the user's choice and outrank the convenience of any particular
use. Entry 13's retained lesson still binds: a tone light enough to read as
light needs ink-coloured text on its fills.

**Type: a serif**, from a system stack — no webfonts, no network payload,
per the house rule KDH states outright. This overrides the Quasar/Roboto sans
default at the app layer, which is established precedent on this dock
(Hotaru and KDH both override the chassis). "or sth very simple" is read as
licence to keep the ramp short rather than as an invitation to a second
typeface.

**"More simple" is treated as a binding constraint, not a mood note** — fewer
tokens, fewer surface steps, fewer bespoke component treatments, restraint
over elaboration wherever there is a choice.

**Microcopy stays plain**, on the reading that "looking" means visual only.
Drafted as `[ASSUMPTION]`; strike it if the register is wanted in the words too.

Delivered to the in-flight distill pass as a mid-run redirect rather than
being applied as a rewrite afterwards.

## 2026-09-15 — Ingredients re-shaped; spines corrected

- **Q2 reversed. Ingredient granularity is now amount + unit + free-text name.**
  The two-level decision of 2026-09-10 (shared category + optional specific) is
  superseded. Requested directly. The blast radius was mapped and confirmed
  before anything was removed: the pantry filter, the shared category
  vocabulary, the coining ceremony, UX-DR7, UX-DR11, UX-DR19's subject, and
  Epic 4's Story 4.4 premise.
- **Consequence for these spines.** `EXPERIENCE.md` lost the Pantry filter
  surface, the New-category row surface and component, the pantry copy-rules
  section, and the pantry state rows; it gained an Ingredient row pattern and an
  Ingredient entry pattern. Flow 2 and Flow 4 steps 5–7 were re-cut around the
  removal. **20 surfaces → 19.** `DESIGN.md` gained an `ingredient-row`
  component.
- **Why the model changed.** The stated reason was that ingredients "always have
  amount + unit + ingredient" — a recipe needs quantities, and the two-level tag
  had nowhere to put them. It also fixed a defect the old model produced: a
  specific that was an adjective (`paprika` → `smoked`) sent **"smoked"** to the
  shopping list. The new shape sends `2 tsp smoked paprika`.
- **What it cost.** The migration is display-equivalent, not lossless: the
  category is gone, so `chicken` → `thighs` is now just `thighs`. Against the
  builder's real collection, 97 ingredient rows migrated and none were dropped.
