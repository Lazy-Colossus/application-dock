- **Stakes read (confirmed by proceeding):** passion/household project — 2 hardcoded users. Right-sized accordingly; no market-sizing/investor rigor.
- **Carried-over decisions from brainstorming (canonical):**
  - Core value: calm, cooperative, low-pressure — NEVER competitive/coercive. No streaks, leaderboards, due-debt, forced challenges.
  - MVP spine: "boring," reliable chapter/topic practice; all playful extras are optional spice.
  - Data model: one shared master vocab list; per-user private progress; words flagged shared/private.
  - Practice modes MVP: Recognition + Production. Listening OUT of scope for now.
  - SRS: automatic/invisible (no due-debt); single visible signal = per-word familiarity color.
  - Cooperative MVP touch: shared human-authored mnemonic note per word.
  - AI enrichment (gap-fill, mnemonic suggest, nuance explainer): DEFERRED to Phase II, but MVP must be ARCHITECTED to accommodate it.
- **Open assumptions to confirm (tagged in brief):** product name; identities/relationship of the two users; vocabulary source/scraping specifics.

- **Name brainstorm:** Explored literal / memory / together / gentle-growth / bookish veins. User chose gentle-growth direction, then bookish-gentle. **COMMITTED: "Hotaru" (蛍, "firefly")** — evokes 蛍雪 (studying by firefly-light); calm, gentle, bookish. All three review assumptions now resolved.
- **POSITIONING RECALIBRATION (review round 2):** "Calm/low-pressure" was over-weighted as the defining feature. Corrected: the **mission is effective, smart learning** — driven by (1) multiple practice angles + active recall, (2) cooperation between users to create memory aids (shared mnemonics), and (3) later AI for tailored examples, word-structure analysis, nuance. **Calm is the manner, not the mission** — demoted to a supporting design principle. Added "word-structure analysis" to the AI capability set. Brief sections rewritten accordingly (Exec Summary, Problem, Solution, What Makes This Different, Design Principles, Scope AI items).
- **Review round 3 tweaks (all accepted):**

  2. **Shared per-word note generalised:** NOT limited to mnemonics — it is a free-form shared notes/light-chat space per word (memory hacks, tips, questions, discussion). Mnemonic is just one use. Reworded everywhere in the brief.
  3. **Grammar and kanji practice:** explicitly placed in **Phase III** (was previously only implied under "out of MVP / broader Japanese learning").
  - Word-structure analysis confirmed as Phase II (point 2 of round 2 confirmed OK by user).
